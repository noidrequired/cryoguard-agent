# MASTER QUALITY AGREEMENT & TECHNICAL SERVICE LEVEL SCHEDULE (GxP)
**Document Ref:** QA-SLA-2026-BV-0489  
**Effective Date:** January 1, 2026  
**Sponsor:** BioVanguard Therapeutics Inc. (Cambridge, MA)  
**Logistics Service Provider (LSP):** Apex Critical Cold-Chain Logistics LLC  
**Product Classification:** Autologous CAR-T Cell Suspension / Biologic Infusion  
**Asset Valuation:** $420,000 USD per patient-specific cryo-cassette  

---

### 1. THERMAL STABILITY & EXPEDITION SPECIFICATIONS
1.1. **Qualified Temperature Band:** Product must be maintained continuously between **+2.0°C and +8.0°C** in transit.
1.2. **Allowable Excursion Budget (Critical Limit):**
   - Cumulative time above **+8.0°C** must not exceed **120 continuous minutes**.
   - Any single spike exceeding **+12.0°C** triggers immediate permanent batch condemnation and mandatory quarantine under 21 CFR Part 211.
   - Cumulative time below **+1.0°C** (freeze risk) constitutes immediate product invalidation.

### 2. DELIVERY COMMITMENT & HOSPITAL SURGICAL PROTOCOL
2.1. **Designated Consignee:** Mayo Clinic Cellular Therapy Processing Facility, Rochester, MN.  
2.2. **Guaranteed Delivery Window (GDW):** No later than **06:00 AM Central Time (CT)** on scheduled delivery date.  
2.3. **Clinical Dependencies:** Patient conditioning (lymphodepletion chemo) is timed precisely to infusion. Delay beyond 07:00 AM CT compromises patient viability and forces surgical cancellation.

### 3. FINANCIAL LIQUIDATED DAMAGES & BREACH LIABILITIES
3.1. **Thermal Excursion Spoilage:** If product is rendered non-viable due to unmitigated excursion (>120 mins >8°C or spike >12°C), LSP is liable for:
   - Full commercial replacement value: **$420,000 USD**.
   - Liquidated regulatory reporting and audit surcharge: **$30,000 USD**.
3.2. **SLA Delivery Delay Penalties:**
   - Delay between 06:01 AM and 07:00 AM CT: **$2,500 USD per 15-minute increment**.
   - Delay exceeding 07:00 AM CT resulting in surgical postponement: Liquidated damages of **$50,000 USD** + 100% freight invoice forfeiture.

### 4. MANDATORY ESCALATION & MITIGATION PROTOCOL
4.1. **Active Intervention Trigger:** In the event of primary reefer compressor malfunction or projected transit delay, LSP is contractually authorized and obligated to execute emergency replenishment (dry ice re-pack or cold-dock diversion) at an FDA-registered, cGMP-compliant depot.
4.2. **Mandatory Notification Window:** Sponsor Quality Desk (`qa-escalations@biovanguard.com`) and Hospital Logistics Coordinator must be formally alerted in writing within **60 minutes** of any confirmed telemetry deviation.
4.3. **CAPA & Deviation Reporting:** Formal GxP Deviation Notice and Initial Corrective and Preventive Action (CAPA) plan must be logged within **4 hours** of incident mitigation.
